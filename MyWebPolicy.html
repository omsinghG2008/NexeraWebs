Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information is collected through this website, how it is used, and how it is kept safe.

What information is collected

When you fill in the booking/enquiry form, the following details are collected: your name, phone number, type of business, the package you're interested in, and any message you choose to add.

How this information is used

  • To get in touch with you on WhatsApp or phone regarding your enquiry.
  • To understand your project requirements before starting work.
  • To keep a record of past and upcoming bookings.

How this information is stored

Submitted details are sent to our business email inbox and are only seen by the NEXERAWEBS team. They are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Your rights

You can request to have your submitted details removed at any time by messaging on WhatsApp at +91 95209 03782 or email nexerametrics@gmail.com.
