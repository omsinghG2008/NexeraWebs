Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

Plans start at ₹1,999 for a 1-page website and go up to ₹19,999 for a complete e-commerce store. See the Services & Features page for every plan.

How long does it take to build a website?

Simple sites are usually ready within a few days. Larger business and e-commerce projects take longer, and you get a clear timeline before work starts.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every plan includes a responsive layout that works on phones, tablets and computers.

Can I add a WhatsApp chatbot?

Yes. It is included in Business Advance and available as a ₹1,000 add-on with eligible plans.

Do you help with SEO?

Yes. Every plan includes an SEO setup, from the default setup in Commercial Basic to a strong setup in Business Advance and E-commerce Advance.
